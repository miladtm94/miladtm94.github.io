Recorded research overview of our IEEE JSAC paper, "Securing Integrated Sensing and Communication Against a Mobile Adversary: A Stackelberg Game with Deep Reinforcement Learning". In ISAC, the better the network senses a malicious target, the better that target can eavesdrop. The talk walks through this security paradox, the Stackelberg game between a trajectory-planning UAV eavesdropper (leader) and a full-duplex ISAC base station (follower), the SCA-based beamforming and artificial-noise design, the double deep Q-learning trajectory planner, and the main simulation results, including about 13% network power savings and robustness to clutter.
